What MetroFeed Is

MetroFeed brings together transit information from multiple sources so you can see what's happening across your system in one place. Instead of juggling apps, tabs, and alerts, you get a clear view of your ride when you need it.

What You Can See

MetroFeed combines the data riders actually check every day, including:

  • Bus and vehicle tracking
  • Rail and subway info
  • Traffic conditions
  • Weather
  • Service alerts
  • System updates

It's designed to help you understand what's happening now, not make you dig for it.

Why It's Free

Core MetroFeed access is free for everyone. We believe essential transit information should be easy to access, easy to understand, and available to riders without barriers.

How MetroFeed Works

MetroFeed pulls together official and public transportation data and presents it in a format built for riders. We focus on clarity, reliability, and speed so you can trust what you're seeing when you check your route.

Where We're At

MetroFeed is actively growing as new features, systems, and cities are added. The platform is continuously improving as integrations expand and more tools come online.

Where We're Going

Our goal is simple: build something riders can rely on every day. Whether you commute, travel occasionally, or just want to know what's happening before you leave, MetroFeed is built to keep you informed.

Feedback Welcome

If you have suggestions, ideas, or spot something we should improve, we want to hear it. Rider feedback directly shapes how MetroFeed evolves.
