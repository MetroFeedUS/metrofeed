About MetroFeed

Software for the real world

MetroFeed is an independent software company focused on transportation, mapping, and public-sector technology.

We build tools that help people better understand the world around them—from public transit and traffic conditions to routing, infrastructure, and real-time information systems.

Our goal is simple: make complex public data easier to access, easier to understand, and more useful in everyday life.

MetroFeed's first public product, RoamRaven, combines transit, traffic, weather, and routing information into a single platform designed to help people move through their communities more efficiently.

As we grow, MetroFeed plans to expand into additional transportation, public safety, and government-focused software solutions.

Our Approach

Public information should be accessible

We believe public information should be accessible.

Many of the systems people rely on every day—transit networks, traffic management systems, weather services, and public infrastructure—already generate valuable data. Our focus is on turning that information into tools that are practical, understandable, and useful to the people who need it.
