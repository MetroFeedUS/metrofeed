Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 16, 2025
Version: 1.2

1. Overview

RoamRaven, operated by MetroFeed (“RoamRaven,” “MetroFeed,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), is committed to protecting privacy and being transparent about how information is handled.

This Privacy Policy explains what limited information may be processed when using RoamRaven and how such information may be used.

RoamRaven may display sponsor advertisements, promotional placements, or related content. RoamRaven does not intentionally use behavioral advertising, personalized profiling, or cross-site advertising systems.

2. Information We Collect

RoamRaven does not require user accounts and does not intentionally collect personal information during normal site use such as names, email addresses, account credentials, payment information, or government-issued identifiers.

With user permission, RoamRaven may access device location information to support map features, routing, nearby services, weather context, and related functionality.

Location information is processed in real time and is not intentionally stored, retained, profiled, or used to create user histories by RoamRaven. Accuracy depends on device settings, browser behavior, permissions, and environmental conditions.

RoamRaven may allow users to save preferences such as favorites, settings, routes, or interface options through local browser storage. Such information remains stored on the user’s device and is not intentionally transmitted to RoamRaven systems.

RoamRaven may use analytics services to collect limited technical information including browser type, device category, pages viewed, feature usage, general traffic patterns, and performance information.

3. Technical Logs and Infrastructure Data

Hosting providers, security systems, infrastructure services, analytics systems, and related technical components may automatically generate temporary logs including IP addresses, timestamps, request information, diagnostics, or error records.

Such information may be used for:

  • Reliability monitoring;
  • Troubleshooting;
  • Performance evaluation;
  • Service security;
  • Operational support.

4. Purpose of Information Use

Information and technical usage data may be used to:

  • Monitor reliability and uptime;
  • Detect outages or failures;
  • Improve design and responsiveness;
  • Evaluate feature usage;
  • Troubleshoot technical issues;
  • Maintain platform security;
  • Improve overall performance.

RoamRaven does not intentionally sell user information or use collected information for behavioral targeting.

5. Cookies and Analytics

RoamRaven may use cookies and analytics technologies for performance measurement, reliability monitoring, and platform improvement.

RoamRaven does not intentionally use analytics information for behavioral advertising or personalized profiling.

Users may disable cookies through browser settings, though certain features may function differently.

Third-party analytics providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

6. External Services and Links

RoamRaven may contain links to external services, websites, maps, public information sources, transportation providers, weather systems, alerts, cameras, or other third-party platforms.

RoamRaven is not responsible for the content, availability, privacy practices, or policies of external services.

Users interact with such services at their own discretion.

7. Data Security

RoamRaven does not operate user accounts, maintain user profiles, or intentionally retain sensitive personal information during ordinary use.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect systems, infrastructure, and services from misuse or unauthorized access.

8. Youth Usage

RoamRaven is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise internet usage by minors.

9. Legal Compliance

RoamRaven seeks to comply with applicable privacy laws and minimize information collection wherever practical.

Users from jurisdictions with additional privacy requirements acknowledge that RoamRaven limits information collection and does not intentionally provide behavioral advertising, personalized tracking, or profiling.

Such information may be used only to:

  • Maintain business records;

  • Meet legal obligations.

Voluntarily submitted information may be retained only as reasonably necessary for these purposes.

Users may request correction or deletion of voluntarily submitted information where applicable.

11. Sponsor and Advertising Information

RoamRaven may accept advertising inquiries, sponsorship requests, promotional materials, campaign information, logos, links, contact information, creative assets, and related business information submitted voluntarily by sponsors, advertisers, organizations, or partners.

Advertising and sponsorship materials remain subject to applicable agreements between the involved parties.

12. User Rights

Users may request clarification, correction, or deletion of voluntarily submitted information where applicable.
