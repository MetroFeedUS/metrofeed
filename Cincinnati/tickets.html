Ticket Purchase

RoamRaven does not currently sell fares or passes. We hope to offer in-app ticket purchases in the future. For now, please buy your fare directly from the transit agency that serves your trip.

Visit an official agency site for fares, passes, and payment options:

  • Visit SORTA / Metro
  • Visit TANK
  • Visit BCRTA

RoamRaven is an independent service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Southwest Ohio Regional Transit Authority (SORTA/Metro), the Transit Authority of Northern Kentucky (TANK), or the Butler County Regional Transit Authority (BCRTA).
