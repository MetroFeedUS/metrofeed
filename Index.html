Cookies and stuff
Know Your App (KYA):

  • Cookies: MetroFeed uses cookies and browser storage for login, preferences, and favorite routes. These help us remember your settings—nothing else.
  • Geolocation: MetroFeed requests your location so we can show you local routes, bus stops, and nearby alerts. Your location is used only in your browser—we never transmit or store it on our servers.
  • Personal Data: MetroFeed does not collect, store, or sell your personal data. Everything—favorites, login tokens, settings—is saved privately on your own device using browser storage.
  • Third-Party Services: Login and payment features may use Google or payment providers. Only the minimum data required to complete login/payment is shared securely with those partners. MetroFeed itself does not store this information.
  • Analytics: We collect basic anonymous usage stats (like visits, clicks, errors) to help us improve the app. No names, emails, or tracking profiles—ever.
  • Transparency: You can review our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use at any time—no need to accept cookies to read them.
Questions or concerns? Email us any time using the “Contact Us” link at the bottom of this page.
