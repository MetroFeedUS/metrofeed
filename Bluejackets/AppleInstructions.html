Add RoamRaven to Your iPhone Home Screen

Step 1: Tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari (square with an arrow pointing up).
Step 2: Scroll the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.
Step 3: Tap Add in the top-right corner. You can rename the icon if you like.
That's it! RoamRaven opens full-screen from your home screen — like an app — and stays on your city.
