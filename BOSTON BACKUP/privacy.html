Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 16, 2025

1. Overview

MetroFeed is committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how data is handled when you use the site. This policy explains what limited information may be processed and how it is used.

MetroFeed may display sponsor advertisements. MetroFeed does not use third-party ad networks or engage in cross-site or behavioral tracking.

2. Information We Collect

MetroFeed does not collect or store personal information such as names, email addresses, account credentials, or payment details. MetroFeed does not require user accounts.

With the user’s permission, MetroFeed may access device location data to support map-based features. Location data is used in real time and is not stored, logged, or retained by MetroFeed. Location accuracy varies based on the user’s device, browser, settings, and environment.

MetroFeed may allow users to save preferences, such as favorite routes, using local storage within the user’s browser or device. This information remains stored locally on the user’s device and is not transmitted to or accessible by MetroFeed.

MetroFeed also uses Google Analytics to collect limited technical and usage information, such as device type, browser type, pages visited, and general usage patterns, in aggregated form.

3. Purpose of Data Use

We use usage and performance data to monitor reliability, detect outages, evaluate which pages are most used, and improve overall design and responsiveness. Data is not used for targeting, profiling, or sold to outside parties.

4. Cookies

MetroFeed uses cookies associated with Google Analytics solely for basic performance measurement and site improvement. These cookies are not used for advertising profiling or cross-site tracking.

Users may disable cookies through browser settings, though doing so may affect how certain features function.

5. Links to External Services

MetroFeed may contain links to external services, such as transit agency websites, alert feeds, or weather maps. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those third-party platforms. Use at your own discretion.

6. Data Security

MetroFeed does not operate user accounts, store sensitive personal data, or maintain user profiles. Analytics data is processed by Google Analytics under their privacy framework. MetroFeed does not retain user-identifiable information on its own systems.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect the site and its services from unauthorized access or misuse.

7. Youth Usage

MetroFeed is not intended for children under the age of 13. While we do not collect personal data regardless of age, our content is designed for general public use and not specifically directed at minors. We encourage parents and guardians to monitor internet usage by minors.

8. Legal Compliance

MetroFeed aims to comply with applicable U.S. privacy regulations to the best of our ability. Users from jurisdictions with stricter privacy laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA) are advised that MetroFeed does not offer personalized tracking or behavioral profiling, and any analytics data collected is used only for performance measurement and site improvement.

9. Voluntary Communication

If you choose to contact MetroFeed (e.g., through our contact form or by email) or submit advertising material, you may voluntarily provide personal or business information such as your name, email address, or promotional content. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry or coordinate advertising services. We do not share this information with third parties or use it for marketing purposes. Advertisers may request removal of their submitted information at any time.

10. Updates to This Policy

This policy may be revised at any time to reflect operational changes, compliance needs, or platform updates. Continued use of MetroFeed after any change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests related to this Privacy Policy, please Contact Us.
