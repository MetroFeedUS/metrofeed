Who We Are

MetroFeed didn't start in a boardroom or with VC money. It started with frustration. Real people (yeah, we're "faceless," but we're here) sick of transit apps that break, lag, or just plain suck.

This isn't some side hustle or school project. It's late nights, weekends lost, patching servers at 2am, and not quitting just because it's hard. It's sweat, stubbornness, and a little bit of "f*** it, we'll build it ourselves."

MetroFeed is for everyone who's ever waited in the rain while two apps lied about the next bus, or tried to plan a trip and got nothing but broken promises and popups. It's for the people who actually *ride*, not just the people in a tech office making slide decks.

What Makes Us Different

Simple: We care more about your bus showing up than about impressing investors. We obsess over live data, map load times, and reliability. If it's not right, we lose sleep and fix it.

We don't have venture capital. We don't have a PR team. All we've got is the drive to make something honest and useful, and we're not afraid to admit when it needs fixing.

Our Mission

To build the transit app we always wished existed. No endless ads, no corporate nonsense... just a real tool for real riders.

What We Stand For

  • Trust riders. Tell the truth. Fix it fast.
  • Keep it fair - never lock up basic info just to make a buck.
  • No BS - if a feature breaks, we own it and fix it.
  • Every city deserves good transit data - Boston is just the start.
  • Don't take shortcuts. Don't sell out.

The Business Side

We won't sugarcoat it... running a real-time, modern transit app takes money. Servers aren't free, live data costs something, and if you want a platform that actually works, somebody's gotta pay the bills. That's us. And yeah, eventually, that means a few dollars from you if you find MetroFeed genuinely useful.

We keep the paywall as light as possible, and we never lock up the basics. You'll always see what you need to catch your ride. But if you want extra features or just want to help keep MetroFeed running, your support means the world and it keeps the lights on, literally.

We're not here to get rich. We just want to build something that doesn't suck and can survive without selling out to ad companies or flooding you with popups.

Where We're Headed

MetroFeed isn't done until every city has a no-BS, live, actually useful transit platform. We're building this thing one city at a time, one route at a time, one frustrated rider at a time.
